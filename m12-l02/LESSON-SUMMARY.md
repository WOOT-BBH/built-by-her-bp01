**Lesson 2 · How do I choose what to grow and set a realistic growth goal?**

Give your next stage a clearer focus. This lesson helps you choose one improvement that matters to your business, recognise useful progress and find a first step that fits the time and resources you have.

You’ll:

- Separate the result you want from the activities you might try.
- Identify the resources and boundaries your goal needs to respect.
- Choose a manageable first step and what it could help you learn.

**You’ll leave with:** a growth or improvement goal note, a useful sign of progress and a practical first step.
