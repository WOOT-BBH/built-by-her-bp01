**Lesson 5 · How do I handle objections and price pushback without pressure?**

A question about price does not mean you need to defend your business or offer a discount. This lesson helps you understand the concern, explain what matters and hold a clear boundary while leaving the customer free to choose.

You’ll:

- Recognise the difference between a question, hesitation and a clear no.
- Prepare useful answers about price, scope and suitability.
- Consider appropriate alternatives and practise a respectful ending.

**You’ll leave with:** a response note for one likely concern, with a clarification question and a boundary you can return to.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
