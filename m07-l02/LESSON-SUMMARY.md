**Lesson 2 · How do I price a service?**

Give your service a clear scope and a price you can explain. This lesson helps you include the work around delivery, compare two sensible ways to charge and keep the main uncertainty visible. You can start with rough figures and leave gaps where you need more information.

You’ll:

- Define what the customer receives, what is excluded and any input you need.
- Count preparation, delivery and follow-up, then make a provisional calculation.
- Compare two charging approaches and draft a clear sentence explaining your proposed price and scope.

**You’ll leave with:** a service-price note and one uncertainty to check before confirming the offer.
