**Lesson 4 · How do I choose which social media or directory profiles to use?**

You do not need a profile everywhere. This lesson helps you consider where a profile could be useful to your customers and what you can realistically keep up to date. Start with familiar options and give yourself room to investigate before committing.

You’ll:

- Identify who a profile should help and what they need to be able to do.
- Compare up to two profile types, separating what you know from what you are assuming.
- Choose a provisional priority and one question to check about fit, rules, costs or practical demands.

**You’ll leave with:** a brief profile comparison and a first option to investigate. Choosing no new profiles for now is a valid outcome too.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
