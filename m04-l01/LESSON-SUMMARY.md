**Lesson 1 · How do I build a brand that fits my business?**

Your brand starts with what people understand and experience when they come across your business. This lesson helps you put that into words, without needing design skills, a new logo or a big budget.

You’ll:

- Describe who your offer helps and what they are trying to do.
- Choose two qualities you want to be known for and how you could show them.
- Write a simple description for your business and decide which choices can wait.

**You’ll leave with:** provisional brand identity notes connecting your offer, the impression you want to create and practical ways to support it.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
