**Lesson 4 · How do I work out which contracts, terms and agreements my business needs?**

Start with one relationship and what each side expects. This lesson helps you make sense of the documents that might support your work and spot the points that need a clearer agreement. You can begin with an arrangement you already have or one you are planning.

You’ll:

- Identify who you are dealing with and what each side promises.
- Understand the different jobs that terms, project documents and other agreements can do.
- Pinpoint an uncertainty and prepare a useful question for a solicitor.

**You’ll leave with:** agreement preparation notes and a priority question to take forward. You do not need to draft legal wording today.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
