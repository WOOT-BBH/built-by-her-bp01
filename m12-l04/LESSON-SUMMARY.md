# Lesson 4 · How do I work out which grants, loans or funding routes may fit my business?

Start with what your business needs the money to do. This lesson helps you make sense of the main funding routes and prepare useful questions, without feeling you have to choose finance or make an application now.

You’ll:

- Describe your funding purpose, rough amount and timing.
- Compare two possible routes or ways to reduce the initial commitment.
- Separate facts from estimates and identify the obligations and risks to ask about.

**You’ll leave with:** a funding-purpose brief and clear questions to take into further research or a conversation with an appropriate adviser.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
