**Lesson 3 · How do I run a sales or discovery conversation that helps both sides decide?**

You do not need a polished script to have a useful sales conversation. This lesson helps you make space to listen, explain what you can offer and agree a next step that makes sense for both sides.

You’ll:

- Set a clear purpose and boundaries for the conversation.
- Choose useful questions and check that you have understood the person’s needs.
- Explain your offer and decide whether to prepare a proposal, clarify something or close the enquiry.

**You’ll leave with:** a discovery conversation outline you can adapt to your business, with questions, boundaries and a possible next step.
