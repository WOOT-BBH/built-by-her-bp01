# Lesson 3 · How do I decide which tasks to turn into a repeatable system?

Some jobs take up more headspace than they need to. This lesson helps you choose one recurring task that could benefit from a clearer way of working, without turning your business into a rulebook or buying another app.

You’ll:

- Compare two tasks and decide where a simple process could make the most useful difference.
- Define when the task starts, what it needs and what a finished result looks like.
- Write four to six practical steps, include a useful check and recognise when to pause for human judgement.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough process note for one task, with clear steps, a place to keep it and an exception route.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
