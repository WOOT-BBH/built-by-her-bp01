# Lesson 5 · How do I use AI safely and know which work still needs a person or specialist?

You do not need to use AI for everything, or have all the answers about it. This lesson helps you decide where it could offer useful support, what information to keep out and when a person or specialist needs to take the lead.

You’ll:

- Sort up to four business tasks by the help and checking they need.
- Recognise what could go wrong and set clear boundaries around the information you share.
- Draft an instruction for one suitable supporting task and decide who must check the result before it is used.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough AI-use boundary, a draft prompt and a clear human check. There is no need to open an account or try the prompt during this lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
