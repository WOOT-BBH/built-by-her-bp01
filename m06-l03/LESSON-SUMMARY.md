**Lesson 3 · How do I create useful content without letting it take over?**

You do not need a constant stream of new ideas to say something useful. This lesson helps you start with one customer question and turn it into a clear explanation, without making content another full-time job.

You’ll:

- Choose a real or assumed question that helps someone make a decision.
- Draft three to five sentences that answer it, explain what it means and offer one next step.
- Choose a manageable format, set a preparation limit and consider where the explanation could be reused.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough content draft, a practical limit and one possible reuse, with any facts still needing checking kept visible.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
