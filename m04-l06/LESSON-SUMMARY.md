**Lesson 6 · How do I keep the brand consistent without creating more work?**

Give yourself a few useful choices to come back to, so every message does not start from scratch. This lesson helps you bring the basics together in a short reference note, using what already works and leaving room for anything still undecided.

You’ll:

- Identify one part of presenting your business that you keep having to rethink.
- Gather up to four reusable choices for your name, description, writing and presentation.
- Write an example sentence and choose an easy place to keep your reference.

**You’ll leave with:** a small brand reference note to use when creating your next message or document. No redesign or full audit needed.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
