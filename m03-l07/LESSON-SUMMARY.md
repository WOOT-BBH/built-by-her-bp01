**Lesson 7 · What product, premises, licence or safety checks apply to my business?**

Get clearer about the checks your business needs before you commit money or begin trading. Start with what you make or do, where it happens and who could be affected.

You’ll:

- Separate the checks for products, premises, licences and safety.
- Identify the official body or specialist who can answer each question.
- Choose which checks need attention before you buy stock, sign for premises or accept customers.

**You’ll leave with:** a dated Operating Requirements Route Map and a clear next action.
