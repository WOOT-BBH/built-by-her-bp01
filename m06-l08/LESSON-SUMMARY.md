**Lesson 8 · Could talks, podcast interviews or press coverage help my business? How do I approach these opportunities?**

An invitation to be seen or heard can sound exciting, but it still needs to work for you. This lesson helps you weigh up who you could reach, what you could usefully share and the time and effort involved. Choosing “not now” counts as a useful decision too.

You’ll:

- Compare two possible routes and consider their audience, value and full commitment.
- Make a provisional choice, set a limit and identify what you need to clarify.
- Draft a short, relevant proposal if useful, or explain why you’re choosing to wait.

**You’ll leave with:** an Opportunity Decision Note, a possible proposal or reason for waiting, and one question to ask before committing. You do not need to find a contact or send anything during the lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
