# Lesson 4 · How do I handle a difficult situation, complaint, return or refund?

A difficult customer conversation can feel personal. This lesson gives you a practical way to pause, understand what has happened and respond clearly, without rushing to a promise or dismissing a concern.

You’ll:

- Draft a calm acknowledgement that avoids blame or assumptions.
- Identify the facts you need and the appropriate guidance or support.
- Set out a clear next step and note one process question to revisit.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough complaint-handling note, a first reply and the uncertainty you need to resolve before deciding what happens next.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
