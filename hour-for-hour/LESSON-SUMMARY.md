**How does Hour for Hour work?**

You have something useful to offer. Explore how exchanging an hour with another member can help you both move forward.

You’ll:
- Explore practical examples of skill swaps.
- Find a category for your offer and understand the boundaries.
- Prepare a clear offer, request and first conversation.

**You’ll leave with:** your own swap notes and a manageable next step.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
