# Lesson 3 · How do I build and use a cash-flow forecast?

See what your bank balance alone cannot tell you. This lesson helps you look at when money is likely to arrive and when payments need to leave, so you can spot pressure early. Start with a few weeks or months and the figures you have, leaving uncertainties visible.

You’ll:

- Sketch expected receipts and payments across up to three periods.
- Calculate closing balances and understand why cash flow differs from profit.
- Try one change in timing or cost and identify a possible response.

**You’ll leave with:** a short cash-flow sketch, one uncertainty to check and a practical question or response to explore.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
