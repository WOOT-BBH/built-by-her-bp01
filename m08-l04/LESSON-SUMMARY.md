**Lesson 4 · How do I write a clear proposal or cost document?**

A clear proposal helps someone understand what they are buying and helps you explain what you are agreeing to deliver. This lesson gives you space to organise the offer, make its boundaries visible and spot what needs checking before you send it.

You’ll:

- Describe what your offer includes and what it leaves out.
- Clarify customer responsibilities, timing and revisions.
- Outline price, payment and acceptance details, with unknowns clearly marked.

**You’ll leave with:** a practical proposal outline and the most important detail or professional check to resolve before sending it.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
