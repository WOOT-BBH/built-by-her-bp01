**Lesson 3 · How do I set up a professional email address and keep control of my accounts?**

Getting help with your email should not leave you dependent on someone else's access. This lesson helps you understand what you need, who should manage it and which questions to ask before setting anything up or making changes.

You’ll:

- Understand the difference between a domain, an email service and website hosting.
- Describe the email arrangement you want and who needs control or permission to help.
- Consider renewal and recovery responsibilities, then choose your first question for support.

**You’ll leave with:** a short email and account-control note containing responsibilities and questions, with no passwords or security details.
