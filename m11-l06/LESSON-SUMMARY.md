**Lesson 6 · How do I know when I will break even, make a profit and be able to pay myself?**

Being busy does not always mean your business is leaving enough for you. This lesson helps you explore what one offer needs to earn, whether the sales required are realistic and what still needs checking before you pay yourself.

You’ll:

- Identify the price, fixed costs and variable costs for one offer.
- Work out contribution and break-even, or identify the figures you still need.
- Compare the result with demand and capacity, and separate profit from available cash.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough break-even note and a clear question about what needs checking before paying yourself.
