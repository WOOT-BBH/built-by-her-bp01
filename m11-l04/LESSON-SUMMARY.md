**Lesson 4 · How do I invoice, record payments and follow up money I am owed?**

Give yourself a clearer way to keep track of money owed. This lesson helps you connect your invoices with payments received and prepare a calm, courteous follow-up when a balance remains outstanding.

You’ll:

- Identify the information your invoice needs and connect it to the agreed work and payment terms.
- Record payments accurately, including part-payments, and check what is still owed.
- Draft a courteous reminder and consider what changes when a payment is disputed or difficult to trace.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough invoicing process, a draft reminder and a clearer idea of when to seek further help.
