**Lesson 1 · How do I work out the minimum price I need to charge?**

Start with one offer and give the numbers some breathing room. This lesson helps you see what a sale needs to recover, including an allowance for your work. You do not need perfect figures or a finished set of accounts. Honest estimates and clearly marked gaps are a useful start.

You’ll:

- Separate costs that follow each sale from ongoing costs and include your own work once.
- Use a realistic sales assumption to make a rough minimum-price calculation.
- Understand what the figure covers and identify the assumption or missing figure most worth checking.

**You’ll leave with:** a minimum-price note for one offer and one clear question to take forward. This is a planning starting point, not your final selling price.
