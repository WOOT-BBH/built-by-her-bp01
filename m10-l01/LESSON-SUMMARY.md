# Lesson 1 · How do I make time for the business while working, caring or managing other responsibilities?

Your business needs to fit into a real life, not an imaginary week where nothing interrupts you. This lesson helps you work with the time you can rely on and make clear choices about what fits now.

You’ll:

- Identify dependable time and match tasks to the attention and conditions they need.
- Choose one useful priority alongside existing commitments and essential admin.
- Decide what to reduce or postpone, with a fallback for weeks when capacity drops.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough capacity note, one clear priority and a realistic trade-off that makes room for it.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
