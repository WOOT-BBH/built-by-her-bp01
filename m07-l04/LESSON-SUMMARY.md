**Lesson 4 · How do I choose the right pricing method for what I sell?**

There is more than one way to arrive at a price. This lesson helps you compare two approaches for one offer and choose a starting point you can explain. Use what you already know, with space to acknowledge what still needs checking. You do not need to settle on a final amount.

You’ll:

- Understand the difference between a pricing method and charging by the hour, project or item.
- Compare two suitable methods, considering your costs, comparable offers and customer benefits.
- Make a provisional choice and identify one check that could change your thinking.

**You’ll leave with:** a pricing-method choice, a clear reason for it and one question to carry forward.
