# Lesson 2 · How do I get my admin under control and build a weekly routine?

You do not need an empty inbox or a new app to make admin feel more manageable. This lesson helps you give important actions a dependable place and build a small routine that works in an ordinary, imperfect week.

You’ll:

- Separate actions you need to take from information you need to keep.
- Decide what needs prompt attention and choose three or four recurring admin steps.
- Keep track of tasks waiting on others and choose one backlog problem to tackle first.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough admin routine, a clear way to capture next actions and a manageable starting point for your backlog.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
