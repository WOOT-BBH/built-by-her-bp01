# Lesson 6 · How do I encourage repeat business and appropriate referrals?

Keeping a customer relationship going does not have to mean discounts or constant messages. This lesson helps you spot when another purchase or introduction could genuinely help, and when good aftercare is enough.

You’ll:

- Identify a real reason for a customer to return, including when a one-off purchase is enough.
- Choose a useful route, such as easier reordering, relevant information or an appropriate introduction.
- Draft a possible message and note the permission, privacy or incentive questions to check first.

**You’ll leave with:** a relationship-development note, a possible follow-up or referral invitation, and one practical improvement to make returning or recommending easier.
