**Lesson 1 · How do I onboard a client or customer so they know what to expect?**

Give your customer a clear, welcoming start. This lesson helps you turn what has been agreed into simple next steps, so they know what happens first, what you need from them and how to get in touch.

You’ll:

- Identify the purchase details that need confirming.
- Set out the first actions for you and your customer.
- Draft a short welcome message with useful expectations and any gaps clearly marked.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough onboarding note and a welcome or confirmation message to refine before use.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
