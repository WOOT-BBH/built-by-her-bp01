**Lesson 7 · How do I build a sales target and know where my next customers will come from?**

Give your sales hopes a practical starting point. This lesson helps you connect a provisional target with the work you can deliver, the customers you might reach and the things you still need to learn.

You’ll:

- Translate a provisional target into orders or projects and check your capacity.
- Separate confirmed work from opportunities and explore how different assumptions change the picture.
- Choose a plausible customer source and one manageable weekly activity.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough sales-planning note that makes your assumptions and next activity clear.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
