**Lesson 5 · How do I check whether my online profiles are doing their job?**

A useful profile helps someone understand your business and know what to do next. This lesson gives you space to review one profile, spot anything unclear or out of date, and choose a manageable improvement. You can work from a live profile, a screenshot, memory or a planned description.

You’ll:

- Check whether your current offer and next step are clear to a new visitor.
- Spot missing or conflicting details and separate what you know from what needs checking.
- Make sense of existing evidence without treating views or followers as proof of sales.

**You’ll leave with:** a short profile review and one useful improvement or unanswered question to focus on next.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
