# Lesson 5 · How do I find useful mentors, networks and introductions?

You do not need a huge contact list to find useful support. This lesson helps you work out what help would make a difference, where you might find it and how to ask in a clear, comfortable way.

You’ll:

- Identify the business question you want help with and the type of support it needs.
- Shortlist one or two people, roles or groups that could be relevant.
- Draft a manageable request, with room for a decline and clear boundaries.

**You’ll leave with:** a support-and-request note and an optional next step, ready when you are.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
