**Introduction · What is the Built By Her Toolkit and how can it help me?**

You do not need to work through everything or have it all figured out. This introduction helps you see what is here and find a useful place to begin, whether you are exploring an idea or already running a business.

You’ll:

- Explore the 12 modules and the questions they can help you work through.
- Understand what you can take from a lesson, and when further advice may be needed.
- Make a short note of your question and what a useful answer would give you.

**You’ll leave with:** your own starting-point note and a directory to return to as your questions change.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
