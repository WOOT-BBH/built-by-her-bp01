# Lesson 2 · How do I separate business money and organise the records I need?

Start with one payment, not a whole pile of paperwork. This lesson helps you follow a sale or expense, connect it to the evidence behind it and choose a way to find that evidence again. You can build on what already works for you.

You’ll:
- Describe where business money arrives and leaves, and how to keep it identifiable.
- Connect one sale or expense to the records that explain it.
- Choose where to keep the evidence and identify a question needing support.

**You’ll leave with:** a proposed route from a business transaction to its supporting records, plus a question to take forward.

**Allow:** 30 minutes. Opening accounts, choosing software and repairing records can wait until later.
