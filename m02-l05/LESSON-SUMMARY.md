**Module 2, Lesson 5 · How do I choose the right business model and route to sale for what I sell?**

Give your offer a practical path to a sale. This lesson helps you choose how you’ll earn income and how customers will find, understand and buy what you sell, with a clear view of what you’ll need to deliver.

You’ll:

- Compare realistic ways to sell and choose one model to test.
- Work out payment timing, delivery and the time, costs or support involved.
- Choose one main buying route and test its least certain assumption.

**You’ll leave with:** a Route to Sale Page and a small test with evidence to look for and a review date.
