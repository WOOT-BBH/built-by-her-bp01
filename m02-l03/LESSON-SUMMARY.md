**Module 2, Lesson 3 · How do I research competitors without getting overwhelmed?**

Make competitor research a useful part of your planning, without disappearing down a rabbit hole. This lesson helps you look at the choices your customers already have and turn what you notice into a clearer next step for your own business.

You’ll:

- Choose up to five relevant alternatives your customer might consider.
- Compare public information and separate facts from assumptions.
- Identify one useful question to explore with customers or further research.

**You’ll leave with:** a Competitor Snapshot Page and a focused research action with a review date.
