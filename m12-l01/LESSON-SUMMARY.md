**Lesson 1 · How do I decide whether to grow, stay small, simplify or move from side hustle to full-time?**

Your next step does not have to mean getting bigger. This lesson gives you space to compare what different directions could offer, what they would ask of you and how well they fit the business you want to run.

You’ll:

- Name the practical result you want your business to provide.
- Compare two realistic directions, including their benefits, drawbacks and commitments.
- Choose a provisional direction or identify what you need to know before deciding.

**You’ll leave with:** a short comparison of two business directions and one useful question to guide your next step.
