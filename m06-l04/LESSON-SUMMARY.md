**Lesson 4 · How do I build and actually use an email list?**

An email list does not need a complicated newsletter or something new to say every week. Start with a clear reason for people to hear from you, a genuine choice to join and a promise you can keep.

You’ll:

- Decide who your emails would help, what you could offer and how often you could send them.
- Draft a clear joining invitation and identify the permission or setup question to check before using a list.
- Write a short first email with one useful point and one relevant next step.

**You’ll leave with:** an email-list purpose note and a rough first message, ready to return to when you work on the setup.
