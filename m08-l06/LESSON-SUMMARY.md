**Lesson 6 · How do I follow up clearly and know when to stop?**

Following up can feel awkward when you are unsure whether to wait or send another message. This lesson helps you make each contact useful, respect what was agreed and give unanswered enquiries a clear stopping point.

You’ll:

- Draft a helpful follow-up with one clear next step.
- Choose whether a reminder is needed and when to stop.
- Write a respectful closing message and record the outcome without guessing.

**You’ll leave with:** a follow-up note with a simple sequence, a final message and any contact-permission questions to check.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
