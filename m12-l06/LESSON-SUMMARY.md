# Lesson 6 · How do I assess a collaboration or partnership before I commit?

A shared idea can open up possibilities, but it helps to know what working together would involve. This lesson gives you space to compare an opportunity, ask useful questions and decide what needs to be clearer before you commit.

You’ll:

- Compare a possible collaboration with a realistic alternative.
- Clarify contributions, customer responsibilities, money and decisions.
- Identify unresolved questions about ownership, information and how the arrangement could end.

**You’ll leave with:** a collaboration comparison and question note, plus a provisional decision to explore further, pause or decline.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
