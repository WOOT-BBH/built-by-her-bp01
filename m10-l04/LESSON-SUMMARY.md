# Lesson 4 · How do I choose tools that will genuinely save time?

A new app can promise a lot. This lesson gives you space to work out what you actually need, compare it with a simpler option and decide what is worth exploring. There is no pressure to buy, sign up or move your business information.

You’ll:

- Name the task you want to improve and the two or three requirements that matter most.
- Compare two realistic approaches, including setup, learning and ongoing work.
- Make a provisional choice and identify one fact or small test that could change your decision.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough tool-choice note, a clearer reason for your next step and a question to check before committing.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
