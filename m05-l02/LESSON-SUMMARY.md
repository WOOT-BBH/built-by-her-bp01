**Lesson 2 · What should my website homepage say and include?**

Help a new visitor understand what you offer without making them work it out. This lesson gives you space to plan clear words, useful details and a next step that fits your business. You can start even if you do not have a website yet.

You’ll:

- Draft an opening sentence and choose clear wording for your main link or button.
- Identify practical details and truthful examples that help someone decide whether to continue.
- Put your content in a useful order and mark what still needs checking.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough homepage content outline you can develop yourself or use to brief someone.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
