Your December Reset

A little space for you. This digital journal gives you a gentle question for each day of December, with room for your thoughts and an optional kind next step.

Open my journal →

There is no challenge to complete. Start anywhere, skip anything and return whenever it suits you. Your only agenda is you.

1. Choose a day

Tap a number in the day picker. All 31 days are available, so you can start wherever you like. During December 2026, Go to today opens the current date. Use Previous and Next day to move between pages.

2. Make a little space to write

Type into the answer box. A word or a sentence is enough. If you feel stuck, tap the small ? or A little help getting started to reveal an optional sentence starter. Tap it again to close it.

The kind next step box is optional too. It could be a rest, asking for support, or deciding something can wait. There is nothing to submit.

3. Come back to your words

Answers save automatically as you type. Look for Saved in this browser ✓. Use the same link, browser profile and device when you return. A small dot under a day means you have written something there. It is a bookmark, not a score.

Open My reflections to see your writing together, then choose Return to this day to edit an answer.

4. Keep a copy

  • Download backup: in My reflections, download a file containing your saved answers. Keep it somewhere private. This is a backup file, rather than a document to read.
  • Print / save as PDF: opens your browser’s print options. Choose Save as PDF where available to keep a readable copy of your written answers.
  • Restore a backup: choose a backup file to move your journal to another browser or device. Restoring replaces the journal currently saved there, so download its backup first if you want to keep it.

5. A gentle year-end reflection

The separate New Year’s Eve page has five optional prompts. You can answer one, try a few or leave them all open. You do not need to list achievements or make resolutions.

Saving and privacy, simply

Your answers stay in this browser. They are not sent to Womeniverse, GitHub or an AI service, and admins cannot read them through the app.

This is not a password-protected diary. Someone using the same browser profile could read the writing. Private browsing, clearing browser data, or using another device can mean your answers are unavailable. Download backups regularly.

The journal link can be opened by anyone who has it. Sharing the link does not share your answers. It does not check Circle membership.

If something does not look right

  • My writing has disappeared: check you are using the original link, device and browser profile. Restore a backup if you have one. Womeniverse cannot recover answers that were only stored on your device.
  • I see “Not saved” or “Saving paused”: keep the page open and download a backup from My reflections. If another tab changed the journal, back up any new writing before reloading.
  • I opened this inside the Circle app: use your phone’s option to open the link in Safari or Chrome, and keep using that same browser. The app’s internal browser may keep separate saved data.

Make it easy to find again

Bookmark the journal link. If your mobile browser offers Add to Home Screen, you can use that too. The journal is a web app and needs an internet connection to open.
