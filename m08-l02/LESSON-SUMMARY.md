**Lesson 2 · How do I respond to enquiries and decide which ones are a good fit?**

An enquiry is a chance to understand what someone needs, without feeling you have to say yes straight away. This lesson helps you reply clearly, ask the questions that matter and decide whether the work fits what you can offer.

You’ll:

- Draft a helpful first response with up to three useful questions.
- Check the request against your offer, timing, price and capacity.
- Practise a polite decline or referral and note a clear next step.

**You’ll leave with:** an enquiry-handling note you can use to shape clear, thoughtful responses.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
