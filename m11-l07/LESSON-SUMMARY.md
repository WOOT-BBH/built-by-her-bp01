**Lesson 7 · How do I create a simple 12-month financial plan and use it to make decisions?**

Give yourself a clearer view of the year ahead. This lesson helps you bring expected sales, costs and cash needs together, so you can explore a business decision with your assumptions in view. You do not need every answer before you begin.

You’ll:

- Outline twelve months, marking known commitments, estimates and unknowns.
- Explore cautious and stronger scenarios, including their costs and capacity needs.
- Identify the assumption or figure that needs checking before you rely on your plan.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough twelve-month money outline and one clear decision question.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
