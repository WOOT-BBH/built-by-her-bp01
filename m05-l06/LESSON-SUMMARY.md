**Lesson 6 · How do I make it easy for someone to enquire, book or buy online?**

Help someone take the next step with confidence. This lesson helps you map a clear route from reading about your offer to making an enquiry, booking or purchase. You can work with what you already have or sketch a route before anything is built.

You’ll:

- Choose the right next action for one offer and identify what people need to know before committing.
- Outline clear steps, ask only for information needed at that point, and explain what happens afterwards.
- Spot one possible obstacle and choose a useful improvement or question to check.

**You’ll leave with:** a short enquiry, booking or checkout route and one priority to take forward.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
