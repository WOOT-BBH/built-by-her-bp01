**Lesson 3 · How do I develop a tone of voice that sounds clear and recognisable?**

You do not need to be a confident writer to make your business sound more like you. Start with one everyday message and turn the way you want to sound into two simple rules you can use again.

You’ll:

- Think about your reader and what they need to understand.
- Choose two writing qualities and turn them into practical rules.
- Draft or improve a short message, keeping the facts and limits accurate.

**You’ll leave with:** two writing rules and a short example message to keep beside you next time you write.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
