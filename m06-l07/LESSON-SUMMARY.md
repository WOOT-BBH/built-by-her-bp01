**Lesson 7 · How do I develop a clear professional point of view and build my reputation?**

You do not need a bold opinion on everything to be known for thoughtful work. This lesson helps you explain one decision you understand, why your approach matters and where it has limits.

You’ll:

- Choose a familiar decision and draw on experience you can honestly describe.
- Connect your view with a practical reason, while recognising claims that need evidence.
- Write a short paragraph and choose one small way your work could demonstrate it.

**You’ll leave with:** a reasoned point-of-view paragraph, an important limit and a possible demonstration. You do not need to publish anything or prove your view is unique.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
