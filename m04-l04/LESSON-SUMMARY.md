**Lesson 4 · How do I write about what I do so the right people recognise it is for them?**

Help someone see what you offer and whether it could be useful to them. This lesson gives you space to turn one offer into a short, clear message, whether you are already trading or still working on the idea.

You’ll:

- Choose one likely reader and the situation they need help with.
- Explain what you provide, its practical use and one accurate reason to consider it.
- Draft two or three sentences with a suitable next step, leaving out unsupported claims.

**You’ll leave with:** a first customer-facing message and notes on any assumptions or claims to check before you publish it.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
