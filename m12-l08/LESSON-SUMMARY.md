# Lesson 8 · How do I prepare to step back, sell, pivot, pause or close the business?

You can prepare for change without deciding everything today. This lesson helps you understand what depends on you, what would need to continue or change, and where a little preparation could give you more options.

You’ll:

- Explore one possible direction and a realistic alternative.
- Identify key owner dependencies, records and commitments.
- Choose one manageable preparation step and a question for appropriate professional advice.

**You’ll leave with:** a future-change preparation note, with a practical first step and a clearer view of what needs checking.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
