# Lesson 7 · How do I protect business data, create backups and prepare for disruption?

If your laptop stopped working or you suddenly could not be there, what would your business need first? This lesson helps you start with the essentials, spot gaps in what you know and think through one practical response without trying to plan for everything at once.

You’ll:

- Identify up to three important records or activities and where authorised access sits.
- Separate everyday file storage and synchronisation from a backup you could actually recover.
- Choose one realistic disruption, a safe first response and the support or information you need next.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough continuity note showing what matters, what is known and which recovery or access question needs attention. No technical setup or password changes are required during the lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
