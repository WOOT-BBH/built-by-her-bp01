**Lesson 5 · How do I work out which tax, VAT, filing and record-keeping tasks apply to me?**

You do not need to become a tax specialist to ask useful questions. This lesson helps you gather what you know about your business, spot what needs checking and prepare for a clearer conversation with a qualified adviser.

You’ll:

- Describe your business structure, activity and stage of trading.
- Identify possible responsibilities and the records that could help you check them.
- Clarify who handles what and write one specific question for official guidance or an adviser.

**You’ll leave with:** a short tax-preparation note bringing together your facts, uncertainties and next question.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
