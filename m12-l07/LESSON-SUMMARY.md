# Lesson 7 · How do I decide whether I need an employee, freelancer, associate or outsourced service?

Feeling busy is a reason to pause and look at what help would actually make a difference. This lesson helps you describe the work, compare realistic options and understand what needs checking before bringing someone in.

You’ll:

- Identify the work, skills, availability and continuity your business needs.
- Compare two support options, including simplifying the work where relevant.
- Consider the full costs, management demands and responsibilities to clarify.

**You’ll leave with:** a people-and-capacity brief and a provisional next step to investigate further, simplify or wait.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
