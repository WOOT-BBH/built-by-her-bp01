**Lesson 5 · How do I introduce my business clearly in a conversation?**

Find a simple way to answer “What do you do?” This lesson helps you put your work into words that feel natural to say, with enough detail to open a conversation and space for the other person to respond.

You’ll:

- Choose one likely listener and what they need to know first.
- Describe your work and who it is for, keeping your business stage clear.
- Draft a short introduction, simplify any awkward wording and keep one extra detail for a follow-up question.

**You’ll leave with:** a short spoken introduction and one useful detail to add if the conversation continues. No recording or rehearsed performance needed.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
