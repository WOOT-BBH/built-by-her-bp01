# Lesson 6 · How do I decide what to delegate and brief someone properly?

Getting help starts with knowing what you need. This lesson gives you space to decide whether one task is ready to hand over, what a good result would look like and what you need to keep oversight of. Simplifying or postponing the work can be a useful choice too.

You’ll:

- Identify whether you need temporary help, ongoing capacity or a specialist skill.
- Weigh the benefit against briefing, checking, time and cost.
- Draft a clear brief with the expected result, limits and decisions that still need your approval.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough delegation brief, a provisional choice and the questions to resolve before appointing anyone. No recruiting or granting access is needed during this lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
