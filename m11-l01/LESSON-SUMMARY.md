# Lesson 1 · How do I work out my startup costs and how much money I need?

Get a clearer picture of what your first step will cost. This lesson helps you look beyond the obvious purchases, decide what can wait and consider the money needed before customer payments arrive. You can start with rough estimates and leave room for what you still need to find out.

You’ll:

- Identify the initial and continuing costs of one small launch or change.
- Separate essential spending from later purchases and optional extras.
- Estimate the money needed, avoid counting costs twice and identify the funding gap or question to explore next.

**You’ll leave with:** an initial money-needed note covering your chosen version, costs, priorities, estimated amount and next question.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
