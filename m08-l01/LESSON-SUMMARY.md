**Lesson 1 · How do I talk about what I do without sounding salesy?**

Talking about your business does not have to mean delivering a pitch. This lesson helps you explain your work clearly, listen to what matters to the other person and suggest a next step that feels useful to you both.

You’ll:

- Write a short introduction in words people can recognise.
- Prepare three open questions and explain an offer that fits what you hear.
- Acknowledge practical limits and invite a next step without pressure.

**You’ll leave with:** your own sales conversation notes, ready to use as reminders rather than a script.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
