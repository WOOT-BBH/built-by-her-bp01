# Built By Her content and voice

Source: *Built By Her — Tone of Voice and Content Guide*, final master edition, August 2026. This is a working summary; the source guide remains authoritative.

## Voice

Trusted, practical, human and forward. Give the useful answer before the explanation. Use plain English, short paragraphs and a specific, realistic next step. Speak to women at different stages and with different ambitions, responsibilities and definitions of success.

Prefer “trusted guidance”, “practical answers”, “a clear next step”, and “build, lead, decide and grow”. Avoid “guru”, “secret formula”, “hack”, “game-changing”, “boss babe”, “girlboss”, “hustle harder”, fear-based pressure and promises of guaranteed success.

## Relationship and boundaries

Built By Her provides practical business information and trusted expertise inside the Womeniverse™. The Womeniverse™ is the wider whole-life membership and resource. Use **Built By Her** in external copy and **BBH** only for internal shorthand or filenames. Avoid calling the Womeniverse™ a platform or subscription in public-facing brand copy.

Frame general content as guidance, not individual legal, financial or other professional advice. Explain the expert's relevance and limits. Do not invent facts about customers, demand or business outcomes.

## Content patterns

- Lesson or guide: open with the outcome, explain the steps, end with a concrete action.
- Social post: start from a recognisable business moment, give one useful insight, then one action.
- Email: state why it matters, provide the useful information, make the response route obvious.
- Event promotion: name the question being answered and the value of attending.

Before publishing, check clarity, practical value, voice, relationship to the Womeniverse™, unsupported claims and whether the call to action is easy to follow.
