**Lesson 1 · How do I decide what online presence my business actually needs?**

You do not need to be everywhere online. Start with what would help someone understand your offer and take the next step. This lesson gives you space to choose what is useful now and leave the rest until later.

You’ll:

- Picture how someone might find you and what they need to understand.
- Consider whether an enquiry, booking or purchase is the right next step, and which online options could support it.
- Make a provisional choice you can maintain, with one assumption or question to check.

**You’ll leave with:** a Minimum Online Presence Note for one offer, including what needs keeping up to date and what can wait. You do not need to create accounts, choose a provider or build a website during the lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
