**Lesson 5 · What information should I prepare before a contract, terms or policy is drafted or reviewed?**

You do not need legal language to explain your business. This lesson helps you gather what you know, describe what happens in practice and make the unanswered questions visible, so a professional can understand the help you need.

You’ll:

- Explain the purpose of one document and the arrangement it needs to reflect.
- Separate current facts, proposed plans, preferences and undecided details.
- Prepare focused questions for professional drafting or review.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough professional brief and a priority question to take forward. You can start here even if you have no existing document or have not completed Lesson 4.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
