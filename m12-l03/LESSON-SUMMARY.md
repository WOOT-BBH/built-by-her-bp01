# Lesson 3 · How do I reduce reliance on one client or income source and test another route?

Make your business less vulnerable to one thing changing. This lesson helps you spot an important dependency and explore a manageable alternative, while protecting the work you already have.

You’ll:

- Identify what your business relies on and what would happen if it changed.
- Compare two possible responses and choose one to investigate.
- Outline a small test, with clear limits and evidence to help you continue, change or stop.

**You’ll leave with:** a dependency-and-test note and a clearer idea of what to learn before making a bigger commitment.
