**Lesson 3 · How do I work out which insurance my business may need?**

Insurance can feel full of unfamiliar terms. Start with the work you actually do and one situation you want to understand. This lesson helps you turn that uncertainty into useful questions for an insurer or broker.

You’ll:

- Understand what the main types of business insurance are intended to address.
- Separate legal requirements, client or venue conditions and your own concerns.
- Describe one possible loss and prepare questions about the cover that might relate to it.

**You’ll leave with:** insurance discussion notes and a clear question to take forward. You do not need to choose a policy today.
