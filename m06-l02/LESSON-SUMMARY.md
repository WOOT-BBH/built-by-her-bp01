**Lesson 2 · How do I build a simple marketing plan for a small budget?**

Make room for marketing without letting it take over your week. This lesson helps you focus on one offer and choose a small amount of useful work that fits the time and money you have available.

You’ll:

- Identify who you want to reach and the response you would like from them.
- Choose one or two actions, allowing time for preparation and replies.
- Set clear time and spending limits, and decide what would help you review or simplify the plan.

**You’ll leave with:** a small marketing-plan note, with manageable actions, clear limits and any unknown costs marked for checking.
