**Lesson 5 · How do I quote clearly and decide on deposits, payment plans and discounts?**

Help your customer understand what they’re paying for, while giving yourself a clearer picture of what you’re agreeing to. This lesson brings the offer, price and payment timing together in one rough quote outline, with room for the details you still need to check.

You’ll:

- Describe what your price includes, what it excludes and how the customer could accept.
- Consider a payment pattern that fits the work and check what a discount would leave.
- Identify the missing details or specialist questions to resolve before sending a quote.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough quote outline, a provisional payment structure and a clear point to check before use.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
