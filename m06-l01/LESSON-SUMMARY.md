**Lesson 1 · How do I choose the right marketing channels instead of trying to be everywhere?**

You do not need to show up everywhere to give your business a chance to be seen. This lesson helps you choose where to put your effort, with your customer, your time and your resources in mind.

You’ll:

- Identify who you want to reach and what your marketing route needs to do.
- Compare two possible channels, including their useful features and practical limits.
- Choose one route to explore, set a time or cash limit and decide what would be a useful sign of progress.

**You’ll leave with:** a provisional channel-choice note, with the uncertainty you need to check clearly marked.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
