# Lesson 1 · How do I choose a business structure and register correctly?

You do not need to settle every legal or tax question today. This lesson gives you space to understand the practical differences between business structures and work out what you need to ask next, whether you are starting out or already trading.

You’ll:
- Compare ownership, responsibility for debts and ongoing administration.
- Identify the registration routes linked to the structures you want to explore.
- Prepare a useful question for an accountant or solicitor, keeping uncertainties visible.

**You’ll leave with:** brief structure and registration notes, plus a question to take forward.

**Allow:** 30 minutes. Optional AI, further reading and follow-up actions can wait until later.
