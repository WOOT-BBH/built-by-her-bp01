**Lesson 6 · How do I know when to review or raise my prices?**

A familiar price can stop fitting the work you do. This lesson gives you space to look at what has changed and consider your options, without feeling you have to announce an increase straight away.

You’ll:

- Review what one offer brings in and what it now takes to deliver.
- Compare keeping things as they are with a change to price, scope or delivery.
- Make a provisional choice and practise explaining it clearly, keeping existing commitments in mind.

**You’ll leave with:** a provisional price-review note, a short draft explanation and a clearer view of what still needs checking.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
