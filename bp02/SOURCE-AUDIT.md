## Deliberate adaptations
- Reused BP-01 logo, colours, type, spacing, labelled inputs, responsive aligned grids, help buttons, disclosure prompts and export notices.
- Ruled spaces become textareas; repeatable Activity 1 starts with one item. Two decision rules are shown initially and the third is optional, matching the source's two-or-three instruction.
- Dates use date inputs. Money and hours remain free text so learners can mark assumptions, periods and varying weeks; no financial calculation or automatic classification is introduced.
- Separate named workbooks remain optional. Each retains its own added items and third rule. No BP-01 draft is read or overwritten.
- Full PDF includes teaching, diagrams, all current fields, expanded guidance/prompts/FAQs and final action. An additional concise statement PDF includes Activity 2, dates, decision rules and least-certain figure. It fits one page with short answers, but expands safely with longer answers.
- Both PDF buttons invoke the browser print dialog; learners must select Save as PDF and check the saved file. Neither button claims success automatically.
- Related future lessons are text references; no invented URLs. No source paragraphs or examples are omitted. Printed continuation headings and fixed page breaks are adapted for a continuous responsive lesson.
