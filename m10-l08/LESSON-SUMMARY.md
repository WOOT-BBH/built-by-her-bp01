# Lesson 8 · How do I manage stock, fulfilment, rotas and busy or quiet periods?

Make room for the work behind each sale. This lesson helps you spot what could slow delivery down and choose a practical change that fits your business, whether you sell products, provide services or deliver something digitally.

You’ll:

- Identify the stage that puts the most pressure on delivery.
- Work out what needs to be ready and which assumptions still need checking.
- Choose one useful change and a fallback if demand or capacity changes.

**You’ll leave with:** a short operational note covering your pressure point, preparation, warning signs, chosen change and fallback.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
