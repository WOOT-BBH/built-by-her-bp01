**Lesson 5 · How do I stay visible to past clients, customers and warm leads?**

People can value your work and still need a useful reminder of what you offer. This lesson helps you find a relevant reason to stay in touch, with room for people to respond in their own time.

You’ll:

- Choose one type of past customer or interested contact and consider what would make an update useful to them.
- Identify a suitable route, keeping any questions about permission visible.
- Draft a short message and choose a sensible occasion and limit for contact.

**You’ll leave with:** a staying-in-touch note and rough message, with one unresolved question to check before taking it further. You do not need to contact anyone during the lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
