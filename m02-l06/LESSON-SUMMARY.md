# Module 2, Lesson 6 · How do I describe what I sell clearly and explain why someone should choose it?

Help the right people understand what you offer and why it might be for them. This lesson brings your customer, offer and evidence together in a short, honest message with a clear next step.

You’ll:
- Describe who your offer is for and what it helps them do.
- Give a specific reason to choose it, supported by proof you can use.
- Write a clear next step and test what people understand.

**You’ll leave with:** a Clear Offer Message Page and a practical plan to test your message.
