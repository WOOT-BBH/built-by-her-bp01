# Lesson 2 · How do I separate personal and business finances and set up simple bookkeeping?

Make your business money easier to follow. This lesson helps you connect payments with the records behind them and outline a manageable bookkeeping routine. You can begin with what you use now, or describe how your proposed business might work.

You’ll:

- Map how money enters and leaves your business, taking your business structure into account.
- Identify the records that explain sales, fees, payouts and owner transfers.
- Outline a simple routine and spot one gap to discuss with a bookkeeper or accountant.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough bookkeeping routine, a clear recordkeeping gap and a useful question for your adviser.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
