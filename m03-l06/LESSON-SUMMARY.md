**Lesson 6 · What privacy, data protection and online trading checks apply to my business?**

Start with one enquiry, booking or order. This lesson helps you see what happens to customer information, spot the questions you still need to ask and choose where to begin. You do not need a website or all the answers to get started.

You’ll:

- Map the information you receive, why you need it and where it goes.
- Separate service messages from marketing and identify questions about tracking or online sales.
- Choose the first uncertainty to take to official guidance or qualified support.

**You’ll leave with:** practical notes about one customer interaction and a clear first question to take forward.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of the lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
