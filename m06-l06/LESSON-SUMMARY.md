**Lesson 6 · How do I ask for referrals in a way that feels natural?**

Asking for a recommendation can feel awkward. This lesson helps you find words that feel comfortable to say, make it clear who you can help and leave everyone free to choose what happens next.

You’ll:

- Recognise when someone has enough experience of your work for a referral request to feel appropriate.
- Describe one type of person or situation you can help, with any important boundaries.
- Draft a short, optional spoken request and give interested people a simple way to approach you themselves.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough spoken referral request and a clear sense of when to use it or hold back. You do not need to ask anyone during the lesson.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
