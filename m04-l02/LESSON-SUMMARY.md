**Lesson 2 · How do I choose values that guide real business decisions?**

Values become useful when they help you decide what to do. This lesson starts with an everyday business situation and helps you connect what matters to you with actions and limits you can work with.

You’ll:

- Choose one or two values that matter to a real or likely decision.
- Compare two possible responses and what each would make easier or harder.
- Make a provisional choice with a practical limit that respects your commitments.

**You’ll leave with:** values in action notes linking your principles to a decision, observable behaviour and a workable boundary.
