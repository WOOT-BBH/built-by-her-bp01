**Lesson 3 · How do I price a product and calculate the margin?**

A product can sell well without leaving much to support your business. This lesson helps you look beyond the selling price, understand what remains and see which costs still need paying. Start with one product and the figures you have. Labelled gaps are welcome.

You’ll:

- Identify the cost of one product, including any allowance for your making time.
- Calculate the amount left and the margin, and understand how margin differs from markup.
- Check what still needs paying and how a discount or different selling route could change what remains.

**You’ll leave with:** a product-price and margin note, plus one missing or uncertain cost to check.
