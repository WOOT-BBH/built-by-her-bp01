**Module 2, Lesson 2 · How do I define my ideal client or customer, specifically, not generally?**

Get clearer about the people you want to serve and what brings them to you. This lesson helps you build a useful customer description from real situations, conversations and enquiries, with space to record what you still need to find out.

You’ll:

- Choose a specific customer group you can recognise.
- Explore their situation, what they want to achieve and how they look for help.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and choose one customer question to test.

**You’ll leave with:** an Ideal Customer Page and a practical plan to test one question over the next 30 days.
