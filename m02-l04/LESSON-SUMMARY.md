**Module 2, Lesson 4 · How do I design an offer people will pay for?**

Turn what you know about your customer into an offer they can understand and consider. This lesson helps you shape a practical first version, set clear boundaries and choose a small test before putting more time into it.

You’ll:

- Shape one offer around a clear customer problem and useful outcome.
- Explain what is included, what is excluded and why the result matters.
- Choose a practical way to test interest or willingness to pay.

**You’ll leave with:** an Offer Design Page and a small test with evidence to look for and a review date.
