**Lesson 3 · How do I deal with work or requests outside what was agreed?**

A customer asks for something more. Before you say yes, quote a price or feel you have to absorb the work, give yourself space to understand the request. This lesson helps you respond clearly while keeping the original agreement in view.

You’ll:

- Distinguish included work, corrections, clarifications and genuine additions.
- Consider how a change could affect your work, cost and delivery.
- Draft a courteous response with workable options and a clear next step.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough change-response note and the checks or agreement needed before you commit.
