# Lesson 5 · How do I ask for useful feedback, reviews and testimonials?

You do not need to ask for glowing praise to learn something useful. This lesson helps you make a simple, thoughtful request that gives customers room to share their real experience.

You’ll:

- Choose between private feedback, a public review and permission to use a genuine quotation.
- Pick a suitable moment and draft one or two clear, neutral questions.
- Decide how to use the response while keeping participation voluntary and publication permissions clear.

**You’ll leave with:** a draft feedback or review request, with notes on timing, intended use and boundaries.

**Keep what you’ve worked on:** export your completed PDF at the end of each lesson so you have your own copy to return to.
