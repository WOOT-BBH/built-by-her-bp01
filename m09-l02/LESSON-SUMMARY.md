**Lesson 2 · How do I plan delivery or fulfilment from purchase to completion?**

Give yourself a clearer route from a customer saying yes to receiving what they bought. This lesson helps you see the steps, spot what could hold things up and make the handover easier for everyone.

You’ll:

- Define what completion looks like for one real or possible order.
- Map four to six delivery stages and identify who or what each stage depends on.
- Choose a useful check and draft a clear response to one possible hold-up.

**You’ll leave with:** a rough delivery route, one practical improvement and notes on how to keep your customer informed.
